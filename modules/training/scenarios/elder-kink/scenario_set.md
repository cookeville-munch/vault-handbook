## Scenario 1: Adaptive Rope Session
**Profile**: 73-year-old practitioner with osteoarthritis in knees and wrists, partners are 35 and 42.  
**Goal**: Practice gentle suspension while accommodating limited mobility.  

**Adaptations**:
- Use chair suspension with weight distribution across 3 contact points
- Replace nylon rope with soft merino wool (lower friction)
- Implement 90-second "pulse check" pauses for respiration monitoring
- Visual consent check-ins using color-coded cards (green/yellow/red)

**Safety Checks**:
- Verify ramp access to venue
- Confirm emergency elevator operation
- Verify assistive device proximity (walker/cane within arm's reach)
- Confirm no recent joint replacement complications

**Aftercare Focus**:
- Extra-long pressure relief for lower back
- Gradual temperature transition (warm blanket → cool compress)
- Verbal processing of bodily changes (e.g., "Notice any new sensations in your hips?")

## Scenario 2: Sensory Play for Cognitive Impairment
**Profile**: 68-year-old participant with early-stage Alzheimer's.  
**Goal**: Experience ritual-based sensation without complex negotiation demands.  

**Adaptations**:
- Pre-agreed sensory script with 3-step progression
- Minimal equipment: warmed basalt stone + textured silk scarf
- Auditory anchoring: 5-min nature sound loop (forest ambience)
- Non-verbal consent: Handheld "green" card for continue, "red" card for stop

**Facilitation Cues**:
- Monitor pupil dilation (indicates arousal level)
- Count respiration cycles every 5 minutes
- Use tactile prompts ("press hand once for yes")

## Scenario 3: Multi-Partner Consent Governance
**Profile**: 3-person dynamic (65, 58, 49) exploring shared submission.  
**Goal**: Establish clear authority relations without overwhelming senior partner.  

**Framework**:
- **Tiered Authority Model**:  
  - Primary Dominant: Sets overall boundaries  
  - Secondary Dominant: Manages session flow  
  - Tertiary Dominant: Handles emergent safety concerns  
- **Consent Validation**:  
  - "Triple Check" system before each escalation  
  - Use tactile tokens (weighted stones) to mark agreement points  
- **Exit Protocol**:  
  - Immediate suspension upon any verbal protest  
  - 15-minute observation period before any re-engagement  

**Documentation**:  
- Record each tier's permission expiration time  
- Log reminder to reassess capacity every 45 minutes
