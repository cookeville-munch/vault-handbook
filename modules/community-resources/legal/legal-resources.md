# Legal Resources Directory

## Overview
This directory contains legal templates, policy frameworks, and compliance guidelines for kink education programs and community operations. All documents follow best practices for risk mitigation, participant protection, and regulatory adherence.

## Core Document Categories

### 1. Consent & Waiver Forms
- **Standard Consent Form**: Template with affirmative consent language, capacity questions, emergency contacts
- **Enhanced Consent Form**: For neurodivergent or low-literacy participants (audio/video options)
- **Group Consent Template**: For workshops with multiple participants
- **Emergency Release Agreement**: Covers unforeseen circumstances and medical intervention

### 2. Operational Policies
- **Community Guidelines**: Behavioral expectations, violation escalation process
- **Code of Conduct**: Zero tolerance for harassment, abuse, and discrimination
- **Mandatory Reporting Procedures**: Steps for reporting suspected abuse or illegal activity
- **Privacy Policy**: Data collection, storage, and sharing protocols
- **Photo/Video Release Forms**: With clear usage permissions

### 2.1 Event-Specific Legal Templates
- **Play Party Agreement**: Outlines rules, consent expectations, and emergency protocols
- **Workshop Liability Waiver**: For educational events with practical demonstrations
- **Mentorship Contract**: For apprenticeship arrangements

### 2.2 Compliance Frameworks
- **Regulatory Checklists**: Mapping to FOSTA/SESTA, GDPR, HIPAA, and local laws
- **Risk Assessment Matrix**: Severity vs. likelihood scoring for activity planning
- **Insurance Recommendations**: Types of coverage (general liability, professional indemnity)

### 3. Advocacy & Policy Resources
- **Legislative Tracking**: Monitoring bills affecting sex-positive education
- **Coalition Building Tools**: Templates for forming advocacy groups
- **Public Statement Templates**: For responding to media or public scrutiny

## Document Standards
- **Version Control**: All files include vX.Y numbering and date stamps
- **Accessibility Requirements**: WCAG 2.2 compliance for digital formats
- **Attribution Requirements**: Community authorship credit and license type
- **Security Protocols**: Encryption for sensitive personal data
