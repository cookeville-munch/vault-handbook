## Community Funding Models
- **Sliding Scale Scholarships**: Need-based tuition adjustments
- **Community Grant Programs**: Funded by member donations and community events
- **Partnerships with Ethical Businesses**: Sponsorships aligned with community values
- **Sliding Scale Membership**: Tiered access based on income levels

## Funding Sources
- **Community Currency Systems**: Time banking or token economies
- **Crowdfunding Campaigns**: For specific educational projects
- **Ethical Merchandise Sales**: Community-designed apparel and tools
- **Workshop Fee Tiers**: With optional scholarship spots
- **Legal Funding Pools**: For legal defense and policy advocacy

## Scholarship Application Process
- **Confidential Needs Assessment**: Anonymous income verification
- **Tier Determination**: Based on income relative to local median
- **Approval Committee**: Rotating community member panel
- **Fund Disbursement**: Direct to educational program accounts
- **Reporting Requirements**: Annual reporting of fund usage

## Industry Partnerships
- **Ethical Supplier Agreements**: Preference for BIPOC, women-owned, queer-led businesses
- **Industry Liaison Committee**: Regular dialogue with ethical manufacturers
- **Revenue-Sharing Models**: Percentage of sales contributed to education funds
- **Co-Branded Educational Products**: With clear community benefit clauses

## Transparency & Accountability
- **Public Financial Reports**: Quarterly summaries of income and expenditure
- **Audited Financial Statements**: Annual independent audit
- **Community Budget Workshops**: Open forums for budget input and review
- **Conflict of Interest Disclosures**: For all committee members and staff
- **Community Oversight Board**: Rotating oversight committee with veto power

## Emergency Support Funds
- **Crisis Financial Assistance**: Grants for sudden medical or housing emergencies
- **Education Continuation Funds**: Support for interrupted programming
- **Travel Grants**: For participants needing to attend workshops or conferences
- **Resource Sharing Pool**: Equipment and material loans to reduce costs

## Educational Resource Funding
- **Open Educational Resources (OER)**: Development of freely accessible materials
- **Community Translation Fund**: Support for translating materials into multiple languages
- **Community-Generated Content**: Incentivized publishing of user-generated guides and tutorials
- **Peer Review Fund**: Support for peer-reviewed educational publications
