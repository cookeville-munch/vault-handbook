## Oral History Interview Framework

### Core Interview Structure
1. **Opening & Context Setting** (10 min)
   - Introduction & purpose statement
   - Consent for recording/use
   - Establish emotional safety signals

2. **Chronological Narrative** (40-60 min)
   - Early community experiences
   - Key turning points/moments
   - Mentorship & knowledge transmission

3. **Thematic Deep Dives** (as needed)
   - Safety culture evolution
   - Consent framework changes
   - Identity & inclusion milestones
   - Crisis response histories

4. **Artifact & Documentation Review** (20 min)
   - Photo albums, journals, newsletters
   - Event programs, zines, flyers
   - Digital archives (forums, wikis)

5. **Closing & Legacy Reflection** (10 min)
   - Key messages for future practitioners
   - Unresolved questions/ongoing debates
   - Hopes for community continuity

## Question Bank by Theme

### Community Formation
- "What brought you into community spaces initially?"
- "Describe the first event where you felt 'this is my place'"
- "How did mentorship work when you started?"

### Safety & Consent Evolution
- "How did safety protocols change during your involvement?"
- "Tell me about a time consent norms shifted significantly"
- "What crisis responses shaped current practices?"

### Knowledge Transmission
- "Who taught you the most critical skills?"
- "What informal learning moments were pivotal?"
- "How did you pass knowledge to others?"

### Identity & Inclusion
- "When did you first notice exclusion in community spaces?"
- "What work toward inclusion felt most meaningful?"
- "How did marginalized voices shape spaces?"

## Documentation Standards
- **File Format**: Markdown with YAML front matter
- **Naming Convention**: `YYYY-MM-DD_interview-subject_theme.md`
- **Required Metadata**: 
  - Interviewer, date, location
  - Subject consent level (public/archived/private)
  - Themes/tags for searchability

## Ethical Guidelines
- **Trauma-Informed Approach**: Pacing, trigger warnings, opt-out options
- **Cultural Sensitivity**: Respect traditions of knowledge-keeping
- **Benefit Sharing**: Community access to archive products
- **Sovereignty**: Subjects retain control over their narratives
