# Toolkit Directory - Essential Templates and Checklists

This directory contains downloadable templates formatted for immediate use, with all necessary compliance documentation.

## Included Templates

## 1. Safety Checklists
- **Pre-Session Safety Checklist** - 
  - Equipment inspection criteria
  - Environmental safety verification
  - Consent verification workflow
- **Post-Session Review Checklist** - 
  - Degradation level assessment
  - Incident reporting completion status
  - Maintenance schedule recommendation

## 2. Consent Forms
- **Standard Consent Template** - With PSD randomization options
- **Enhanced Consent Form** - With auditory/visual alternatives
- **Emergency Release Agreement** - With legal clarity provisions

## 3. Aftercare Guides
- **Personal Aftercare Plan Worksheet** - For participants to track needs
- **Aftercare Support Letter Template** - For community assistance coordination
- **Self-Healing Resource List** - With professional referral suggestions

## 4. Incident Reporting
- **Incident Report Template** - With mandatory fields and privacy considerations
- **Debrief Summary Form** - For practitioners to document learning

## 5. Equipment Maintenance
- **Suspension Cable Inspection Log** - With periodic testing schedule
- **Hardware Maintenance Schedule** - With service interval recommendations
- **Equipment Inventory Tracker** - With location and condition tracking

*All templates include:*
- Version tracking (v1.0-v2.1)
- Consistent contributor attribution requirements
- Standardized file naming conventions
- Compliance annotations indicating legal considerations
- Community-specific accessibility tags
- NVIDIA-generated-2024 header for digital provenance
