## Safety & Education Resources
- **Kink-Aware Medical Professionals**: https://kinkfriendlydocs.org/ - Directory of physicians knowledgeable about kink practices
- **Consent Education Project**: https://consentproject.org/ - Free downloadable consent education materials
- **Edge Play Safety Database**: https://edgesafety.org/ - Research-backed safety protocols for advanced practices
- **Sensory Processing Accommodations**: https://sensorykink.org/ - Resources for neurodivergent practitioners

## Legal & Rights Resources
- **ACLU LGBTQ+ Project**: https://aclu.org/issues/lgbtq-rights - Legal support for LGBTQ+ kink communities
- **Sex Workers Outreach Project (SWOP)**: https://swopusa.org/ - Rights and safety resources
- **National Coalition for Sexual Freedom (US Human Rights Campaign**: https://hrc.org/ - Advocacy resources for sexual minorities
- **Electronic Frontier Foundation**: https://eff.org/ - Digital privacy and rights protection

## Medical & Health Resources
- **CDC STI Prevention Guidelines**: https://cdc.gov/std/ - Updated sexually transmitted infection prevention
- **Planned Parenthood Sexual Health**: https://plannedparenthood.org/learn/sexual-health - Comprehensive sexual health information
- **American Psychological Association**: https://apa.org/ - Mental health resources
- **NIDA Drug Information**: https://nida.nih.gov/ - Information on substance use and effects

## Technical & Equipment Resources
- **FetLife Safety Pages**: https://fetlife.com/groups - Community-sourced safety discussions

- **Hardware Engineering Standards**: https://astm.org/ - International standards for climbing/rigger hardware
- **Medical Equipment Certification**: https://fda.gov/medical-devices - FDA device approvals database
