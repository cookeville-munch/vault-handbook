## Tiers of Crisis Management
1. **Tier 1: Minor Incident**
   - Verbal de-escalation
   - Temporary withdrawal of participants
   - Situation documentation

2. **Tier 2: Moderate Crisis**
   - Scene containment procedures
   - Medical assessment protocols
   - Mandatory reporting requirements

3. **Tier 3: Major Emergency**
   - Full emergency services activation
   - Evacuation procedures
   - Mass casualty triage implementation

## Response Team Roles
- **Incident Commander**
  - Overall situation assessment
  - Resource allocation decisions
  - Communication with external authorities

- **Safety Officer**
  - Hazard identification
  - Personal protective equipment distribution
  - Environmental safety verification

- **Medical Officer**
  - Triage assessment
  - First aid administration
  - Patient transport coordination

- **Documentation Officer**
  - Incident report generation
  - Witness statement collection
  - Evidence preservation procedures
