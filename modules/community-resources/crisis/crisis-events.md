# Crisis Event Management for Play Parties

## Emergency Response Structure

### First Response Team (3-5 members)
- **Team Lead**: Coordinates response, communicates with venue management
- **Medical Liaison**: First aid/CPR certified, manages medical emergencies
- **Emotional Support Lead**: Trained in crisis de-escalation, manages affected participants
- **Safety Officer**: Monitors scene safety, coordinates evacuation if needed
- **Documentation Lead**: Records incident details, maintains confidentiality

### Activation Protocol
1. **Recognition**: Any team member identifies potential crisis
2. **Signal**: Discrete hand signal or code word activates team
3. **Assessment**: Rapid evaluation (physical safety, emotional state, scope)
4. **Intervention**: Appropriate response per crisis type
5. **Documentation**: Complete incident report within 1 hour

## Crisis Types & Responses

### Medical Emergencies
- **Immediate**: Call 911, provide first aid, clear area
- **Support**: Emotional support for witness participants
- **Follow-up**: Hospital liaison, community notification if needed

### Consent Violations
- **Immediate**: Separate parties, ensure safety of affected person
- **Assessment**: Private conversation with trained mediator
- **Options**: Mediation, temporary separation, community accountability process
- **Documentation**: Detailed confidential report

### Psychological Crises
- **Immediate**: Quiet space, grounding techniques, peer support
- **Assessment**: Suicide/self-harm risk evaluation by trained responder
- **Referral**: Crisis line, therapist, emergency services if high risk
- **Aftercare**: 24/48/72 hour check-ins

### Environmental Hazards
- **Fire/Structural**: Immediate evacuation per venue plan
- **Equipment Failure**: Secure area, assess injuries, replace equipment
- **Substance Overdose**: Naloxone administration, recovery position, EMS

## Vicarious Trauma Mitigation
- **Debrief Requirement**: Mandatory team debrief within 2 hours
- **Rotation Policy**: Maximum 2-hour crisis response shifts
- **Professional Support**: Access to therapist for responders
- **Peer Check-ins**: Daily check-ins for 72 hours post-event

## Community Communication
- **During Event**: Discrete updates to organizers only
- **Post-Event**: General safety announcement (no identifying details)
- **Follow-up**: Resource email to all attendees within 24 hours
- **Review**: Policy review within 2 weeks
