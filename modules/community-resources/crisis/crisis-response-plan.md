## Core Response Framework
1. **Initial Assessment**
   - Safety check protocol
   - Medical evaluation start
   - Emotional state check

2. **Immediate Actions**
   - De-escalation team dispatch
   - Medical emergency handling
   - Scene safety isolation

3. **Post-Crisis Support**
   - Trauma first aid
   - Long-term support connections
   - Community healing resources

## Role Assignments
- Safety Officer: Scene manager & hazard control
- Medical Officer: First aid & triage
- Documentation Officer: Incident reporting
- Community Liaison: External communication

## Escalation Protocol
- Level 1: Facility self-management
- Level 2: Call local emergency services
- Level 3: Activate regional crisis network
