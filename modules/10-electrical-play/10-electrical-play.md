# Electrical Play

**Level: Advanced** | **Module: 10-electrical-play** | **Prerequisites: 01-orientation-consent, 02-session-techniques**

Electrical Play introduces the safe and consensual use of electricity in kink scenarios. It covers device types, physiological effects, risk mitigation, and scene planning to enable practitioners to explore electro‑stimulation responsibly.

## Overview

Electrical Play provides a structured framework for incorporating low‑current devices such as violet wands, TENS units, and conductive toys into scenes. The module emphasizes informed consent, medical screening, equipment inspection, and emergency procedures. It builds on consent negotiation, technical safety, and instructional design principles from prerequisite modules.

## Key Terms

**Electro‑stimulation**: Application of controlled electrical current to the body for sensory or muscular response.
**Violet Wand**: High‑frequency, low‑current device producing visible arcs used for surface stimulation.
**TENS Unit**: Transcutaneous Electrical Nerve Stimulation device delivering adjustable pulses for therapeutic or erotic sensation.
**Current Path**: The route electricity takes through the body; must avoid crossing the chest or head.
**Grounding**: Ensuring a safe return path for current to prevent unintended flow.

## Practice Examples

### Example 1: Violet Wand Introduction

A practitioner demonstrates a violet wand on a consenting partner's forearm, explaining sensation gradients, safe distance, and immediate stop signals. The scene includes pre‑scene medical screening for cardiac conditions and a dedicated spotter.

### Example 2: TENS Unit Sensation Mapping

Partners use a dual‑channel TENS unit to map erogenous zones, adjusting frequency and amplitude while maintaining continuous verbal check‑ins. Electrodes are placed below the waist to avoid cardiac current paths.

### Example 3: Conductive Rope Integration

A rope bondage scene incorporates conductive rope connected to a low‑current controller, allowing the top to modulate sensation through tension changes. Emergency release shears are staged within reach.

## Common Challenges

1. **Medical Contraindications**: Participants may have undisclosed implants or cardiac history. *Solution:* Mandatory pre‑scene health questionnaire and physician clearance for high‑risk individuals.
2. **Device Malfunction**: Faulty wiring or battery failure can cause unexpected current spikes. *Solution:* Pre‑scene equipment test with multimeter; maintain spare batteries and backup device.
3. **Sensation Overload**: Rapid escalation can exceed tolerance, leading to panic or injury. *Solution:* Structured ramp‑up protocol with timed intervals and mandatory pause points.

## Learning Objectives

1. Identify appropriate electro‑stimulation devices for given experience levels.
2. Conduct thorough risk assessments including medical screening and environment inspection.
3. Design and negotiate electro‑play scenes with explicit consent, safe‑word, and emergency plans.
4. Demonstrate proper electrode placement, current path awareness, and device operation.
