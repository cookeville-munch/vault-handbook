# Skill Tracker Framework

**Level: Foundational**
## Learning Objectives

1. **Understand Skill Tracker Framework** - Explain the core concepts and significance of this topic within the kink training framework
1. **Apply Skill Categories** - Demonstrate practical understanding and implementation of skill categories
1. **Identify key safety considerations** - Recognize and articulate important safety protocols
1. **Apply best practices** - Demonstrate correct implementation of learned techniques

 | **Module: participant-materials** | **Prerequisites: 01-orientation-consent**


## Key Takeaways

- **Skill Categories** - Understanding and applying skill categories is essential for safe and effective practice
- **Safety First** - Always prioritize safety protocols in all activities
- **Communication is Key** - Clear, ongoing communication ensures consent and safety
- **Practice Makes Progress** - Regular practice builds confidence and competence

## Skill Categories
**A. Technical Skills**
- Basic impact techniques (single arm, partner, flying)
- Advanced bondage (basic, intermediate, complex)
- Sensation play (heat, cold, vibration, pressure)
- Position play (static, dynamic, suspension)

**B. Relational Skills**
- Negotiating hard/soft limits
- Creating consent agreements
- Communication cue systems
- Aftercare planning
- Body positivity modeling

**C. Safety & Risk Management**
- Scene safety protocols
- Equipment inspection standards
- Emergency response procedures
- Medical awareness (common conditions)
- Risk-benefit assessment

**D. Educational Skills**
- Audience engagement techniques
- Accessibility accommodations
- Cultural competency considerations
- Trauma-informed facilitation
