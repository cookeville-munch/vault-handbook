# First Aid Training & Resources

**Level: Foundational**
## Learning Objectives

1. **Understand First Aid Training & Resources** - Explain the core concepts and significance of this topic within the kink training framework
1. **Apply Core First Aid Protocol** - Demonstrate practical understanding and implementation of core first aid protocol
1. **Apply Training Components** - Demonstrate practical understanding and implementation of training components
1. **Apply Resources** - Demonstrate practical understanding and implementation of resources

## Key Takeaways

- **Core First Aid Protocol** - Understanding and applying core first aid protocol is essential for safe and effective practice
- **Training Components** - Understanding and applying training components is essential for safe and effective practice
- **Resources** - Understanding and applying resources is essential for safe and effective practice

## Core First Aid Protocol
1. **Assessment Sequence**
   - Scene safety check
   - Responsiveness assessment
   - Airway maintenance
   - Breathing evaluation
   - Circulation assessment

2. **Emergency Procedures**
   - Venous air embolism response
   - Nerve compression protocols
   - Positional asphyxia recognition
   - Shock management guidelines

3. **Equipment Standards**
   - Kit contents checklist
   - Maintenance schedules
   - Expiration tracking

## Training Components
- Basic life support (BLS) for kink contexts
- Scene-specific emergency drills
- Post-incident care coordination
- Documentation best practices

## Resources
- Emergency contact templates
- Medical information forms
- Incident report templates
- Follow-up care protocols
