## Finding Your Mentor
1. **Online Search**
   - Browse community forums and social media
   - Join support groups (online & local)
   - Search professional directories

2. **Referral System**
   - Ask at workshops/
   - Check with your facilitator
   - Request community recommendations

3. **Virtual Connections**
   - Consider mentors in different regions
   - Use video conferencing for initial meetings
   - Research online mentor credentials

## Relationship Setup
### Establishing Boundaries
- Define session expectations
- Set frequency and timing
- Agree on communication methods
- Discuss emergency protocols

### Consent & Negotiation
- Personal style exploration
- Priority areas (areas needing growth)
- Comfort levels in learning
- Trust building activities

### Session Structure
1. **Welcome & Check-in**
   - Recite agreed-upon rules
   - assess energy states

2. **Skill Practice**
   - Primary skills practice
   - Transfer to scenarios

3. **Feedback & Review**
   - What went well?
   - What could improve?
   - Next steps

## Communication Guidelines
- **Message Timing**: 24 hours advance notice
- **Flexibility**: Adjustable dates for emergencies
- **Language**: Clear, specific, non-judgmental
- **Methods**: Phone, video, in-person (whichever agreed)

## Progress Tracking
- **Weekly Journal**: Document activities, insights, challenges
- **Monthly Goals**: Set specific, measurable objectives
- **Quarterly Review**: Reassess and reset objectives

## Managing Sessions
### What to Bring
- Learning materials from mentor
- Previous journal entries
- Question list
- Any necessary equipment

### During Sessions
- Be present (no distractions)
- Practice consistently
- Take notes
- Ask for clarification

### After Sessions
- Review what was learned
- Practice new skills with safe person
- Reflect on experience
- Submit journaling

## Growth & Success
- Celebrate small wins
- Accept mistakes as learning
- Allow yourself to be vulnerable
- Celebrate consistent practice

## Professional Concerns
- Conflict resolution
- Distance learning
- Credential verification
- Emergency support

## Future Planning
- When to seek new mentor
- What skills to next seek
- Joining mentor community
- Teaching others

## Quality Resources
- **Online**: Community forums, video courses
- **Books**:Consent guide, boundary books, kink education resources
- **Local**: Workshops, meetups, community spaces

This guide should be personalized based on your individual needs, goals, and comfort levels.
