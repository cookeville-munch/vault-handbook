# Sensation Play Methodologies  
## Core Principles  
- **Sensory Hierarchy**: Understanding physiological responses to different textures (e.g., silk vs. rubber)  
- **Threshold Management**: Techniques to safely escalate or de-escalate sensation intensity  
- **Emotional Mapping**: Connecting physical sensations to emotional states  

## Material Selection Guide  
- **Soft Touch**: Silicone tools for gentle friction (ideal for hypersensitive participants)  
- **Tactile Variety**: Rough surfaces (ropes), vibrating implements (for hyposensitive learners)  
- **Temperature Play**: Controlled hot/cold applications with safety check points  

## Technique Workflows  
- **Progressive Build Technique**:  
  1. Establish baseline comfort with light touch  
  2. Introduce rhythmic stimulation  
  3. Add intensity with targeted pressure points  
  4. Incorporate variable texture sequences  
- **Sensory Deprivation Integration**:  
  - Combine tickling with blindfolding (requires pre-negotiated safety)  
  - Use temperature shifts during suspension  

## Assessment Criteria  
- **Technical**: Correct material use, adherence to negotiation protocols  
- **Ethical**: Respect for emerging boundaries, aftercare initiation  
- **Emotional**: Student-reported sense of agency during play
