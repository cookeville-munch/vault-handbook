## Chapter 2: Connected Device Ecosystem Security

### 2.1 Internet of Things (IoT) Intimate Devices
- **Communication Protocol Security**:
  - End-to-end encryption for all device-to-device and device-to-cloud
  - Mutual authentication preventing man-in-the-middle attacks
  - Firmware signing with hardware-rooted trust
- **Physical Safety Interlocks**:
  - Mechanical hardstops independent of electronic controls
  - Pressure/force sensors with immediate power cutoff
  - Temperature monitoring with thermal runaway protection
- **Data Minimization Principles**:
  - On-device processing whenever possible
  - Ephemeral session data with automatic secure deletion
  - User-controlled data sharing permissions

### 2.2 Teledildonics & Remote Play Systems

## Chapter 4: Digital Identity & Privacy Preservation

### 4.1 Pseudonymity & Identity Management
- **Selective Disclosure Systems**:
  - Zero-knowledge proofs for attribute verification (age, experience level)
  - Contextual identity presentation (different personas for different spaces)
  - Revocable pseudonyms with unlinkability guarantees
- **Relationship Graph Protection**:
  - Encrypted social graphs with differential privacy
  - Contact tracing prevention through rotating identifiers
  - Community detection resistance through community mixing
- **Legacy & Emergency Access**:
  - Pre-arranged emergency contact protocols with lawyer oversight
  - Digital wills for online persona management
  - Trusted recovery systems with Shamir's Secret Sharing

### 4.2 Surveillance Resistance & Anti-Tracking
- **Fingerprinting Mitigation**:
  - Canvas/webgl/API spoofing with consistent entropy
  - Font and plugin enumeration normalization
  - Audio/video device fingerprint randomization
- **Location Privacy Enhancements**:
  - Differential privacy for geolocation check-ins
  - Fuzzing with user-controlled precision levels
  - Venue-level vs. precise coordinate options
- **Network Obfuscation Techniques**:
  - Tor integration with bridge selection for restrictive regions
  - VPN chaining with jurisdictional separation
  - Traffic shaping to resist correlation attacks

### 4.3 Data Lifecycle & Digital Afterlife
- **Ephemeral by Default Design**:
  - Message expiration with configurable timers (seconds to years)
  - Screen-safe viewing modes to prevent shoulder surfing
  - Remote wipe capabilities for lost/stolen devices
- **Digital Legacy Planning**:
  - Inactive account protocols with trusted contact notification
  - Memorialization options with consent verification
  - Data donation frameworks for research with ethics oversight
- **Right to be Forgotten Implementation**:
  - Comprehensive data erasure across all storage systems
  - Backup and archive purge procedures with verification
  - Third-party data processor notification and compliance tracking
