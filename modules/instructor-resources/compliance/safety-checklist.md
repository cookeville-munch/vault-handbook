## Key Takeaways

- **Regulatory Standards** - Understanding and applying regulatory standards is essential for safe and effective practice
- **Implementation Phases** - Understanding and applying implementation phases is essential for safe and effective practice
- **Monitoring Systems** - Understanding and applying monitoring systems is essential for safe and effective practice
- **Key Compliance Areas** - Understanding and applying key compliance areas is essential for safe and effective practice

## Regulatory Standards
1. **Workplace Safety**
   - OSHA/ANSI compliance markers
   - Equipment certification logs
   - Incident reporting forms

2. **Community Standards**
   - Consent documentation requirements
   - Privacy protection measures
   - Inclusion policy enforcement

## Implementation Phases
1. **Documentation**
   - Policy manuals (digital/print)
   - Audit trails & version control
   - Training records

2. **Verification**
   - Monthly self-assessment
   - Quarterly external review
   - Annual certification renewal

## Monitoring Systems
- Compliance scorecards
- Non-conformance tracking
- Corrective action logs

- [ ] Physical safety (equipment, spaces)
- [ ] Emotional safety (consent, boundaries)
- [ ] Digital security (data, privacy)
- [ ] Legal requirements (local/regional)
