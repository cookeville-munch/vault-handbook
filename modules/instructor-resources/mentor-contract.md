## Competency Milestones (UPDATED)
**Phase 1 - Foundation (Months 1-2):**
- Core technique fundamentals
- Basic safety protocol compliance
- Inclusive communication basics
- **Visual Safety Literacy: Flowchart interpretation basics (carabiner, rope, hardware)**

**Phase 2 - Integration (Months 3-4):**
- Adaptive technique application
- Cultural humility in practice
- **Visual Safety Literacy: Scene layout design, multi-modal safety communication**
- **Accessible Visual Design: High-contrast and tactile diagram creation basics**

**Phase 3 - Mastery (Months 5-6):**
- Independent session facilitation
- Peer mentoring capability
- **Visual Safety Literacy: Teaching flowchart interpretation, accessible design mentorship**
- **Innovation Project: Develop inclusive visual safety material for community use**

## Responsibilities

### Mentor Duties
- **Education & Training**: Provide structured curriculum covering core competencies (including inclusive practice milestones AND visual safety literacy competencies)
- **Safety Oversight**: Monitor practice sessions for safety compliance (inclusive of accessibility and sensory needs, visual safety assessment accuracy)
- **Boundary Guidance**: Model healthy boundary setting and negotiation (including neurodivergent and disability-affirming models)
- **Mentorship Hours**: Commit to minimum of 4 hours/month direct supervision
- **Feedback Provision**: Provide constructive feedback within 48 hours of observations (using growth-oriented, inclusive language)
- **Ethical Guidance**: Strict adherence to community ethical guidelines and cultural humility frameworks
- **Confidentiality**: Maintain confidentiality of apprentice's personal information
- **Resource Referral**: Direct apprentice to relevant educational materials and support services (including identity-specific resources AND visual safety toolkit)

### Apprentice Duties
- **Practice Activities**: Execute learned techniques under supervision
- **Compliance**: Follow all safety protocols and ethical guidelines
- **Documentation**: Keep accurate logs of practice sessions and learning progress (including accessibility adjustments used AND visual safety assessment practice logs)
- **Feedback Acceptance**: Receive and implement mentor feedback
- **Confidentiality**: Maintain confidentiality of mentor's personal information
- **Growth Focus**: Complete minimum 10 hours of practice per month
- **Community Contribution**: Participate in community service or educational events (including inclusive outreach AND visual safety literacy workshops)
- **Visual Safety Competency**: Achieve proficiency in all four equipment inspection flowcharts (carabiner, rope, hardware, scene layout) and demonstrate accessible design creation

## Code of Conduct
- **Professional Boundaries**: No romantic/sexual relationship with mentee
- **Conflict of Interest**: Avoid financial arrangements with mentee
- **Harm Prevention**: Zero tolerance for abuse, harassment, or exploitation
- **Knowledge Sharing**: Mentor will share educational resources freely
- **Respect**: Demonstrate respect for self, others, and community norms (inclusive of all neurotypes and abilities)
- **Visual Safety Integrity**: Maintain accuracy and accessibility of all visual safety materials

## Termination Conditions
- **Mutual Agreement**: Both parties agree to terminate
- **Breach of Code**: Immediate termination for foundation violation
- **Inactivity**: 3 consecutive months of no contact triggers review
- **Program Expiration**: Automatic termination after 12-month term
- **Competency Failure**: Failure to achieve Phase 2 visual safety literacy milestones triggers remedial plan

## Amendment Process
- **Mutual Written Agreement**: All changes must be documented in writing
- **Notice Period**: 14 days minimum before amendments take effect
- **Oversight Review**: Community Ethics Committee approval required for significant changes
