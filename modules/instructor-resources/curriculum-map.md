# Curriculum Map for Kink Education Program

## eight-week progressive learning schedule
**Week 1-2**: Foundations of Consent & Safety
- Core readings: Consent principles, trauma-informed care
- Practical: Basic negotiation exercises, safety protocol drills

**Week 3-4**: Technical Skills Foundations
- Rope suspension basics, medical play fundamentals
- Lab: Equipment inspection, basic tie-tying

**Week 5-6**: Advanced Practice Techniques
- Sensation play choreography, advanced restraint methods
- Role-play: High-intensity scenario simulations

**Week 7-8**: Community Leadership & Ethics
- Mentorship frameworks, public education design
- Final project: Design a community workshop

## Competency Thresholds
- Level 1: 90%+ completion of prerequisite modules
- Level 2: Demonstrated proficiency in 3 core techniques
- Level 3: Successful mentorship of 2+ newcomers
- Level 4: Peer-reviewed curriculum contribution

## Inter-Module Dependencies
- Completion of Module 1 required before Module 2
- Module 3 requires documented safety audit
- Module 4 requires 10 hours of community service

## Assessment Integration
- Weekly knowledge quizzes (auto-generated)
- Monthly practical evaluations (peer-reviewed)
- Final portfolio review (community panel)
