## Accessibility Adaptations
1. **Mobility Modifications**
   - Position adjustments (sitting/standing options)
   - Equipment selection (lighter materials)
   - Support device integration

2. **Cognitive Support**
   - Visual aids for complex protocols
   - Audio instructions for navigation
   - FAQ guides for common questions

3. **Sensory Adjustments**
   - Lighting/volume controls
   - Alternative tactile cues
   - Stress reduction techniques

## Cultural Adaptations
- Decolonized practice frameworks
- Language localization guidelines
- Tradition preservation protocols
