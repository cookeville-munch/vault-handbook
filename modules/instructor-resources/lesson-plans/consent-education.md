# Lesson Plan Template: Consent Education for New Practitioners

## Core Principle Framework
- **Affirmative Consent = Ongoing Affirmative Consent**
- **No Assumptions = No Pressures**
- **"Your Kink Is Not My Kink And That's Okay" Integration**

## Session Breakdown

### 1. Introduction (45 minutes)
- **Warm-Up**: "Judgment-Free Fantasy Sharing" (communicate desires without approval)
- **Video Analysis**: Diverse consent negotiation examples (watch & discuss)
- **Role-Play**: Consent negotiation scenarios (rotating partners)
- **Debrief**: Key takeaways, common pitfalls

### 2. Skills Practice (90 minutes)
- **Skill Builder**: Consent agreement drafting (with rubric feedback)
- **Dynamic Practice**: Consent check-ins during simulated play
- **Boundary Mapping**: Visual mapping of personal/partner boundaries
- **Digital Consent**: Email/text consent templates practice

### 2. Observation & Feedback (60 minutes)
- **Live Observation**: Watch experienced mentor negotiate with participant
- **Feedback Circle**: Peer and mentor feedback using structured rubric
- **Boundary Innovation**: Practice creative boundary communication techniques
- **Self-Assessment**: Update personal learning journal

## Instructor Toolkit
- **Consent Script Gallery**: Sample dialogues for varied scenarios
- **Boundary Mapping Template**: Visual mapping worksheet
- **Consent Negotiation Checklist**: Step-by-step facilitation guide
- **Crisis Response Playbook**: For boundary violations or emergencies
- **Escalation Protocols**: Clear escalation paths for different violation types

## Evaluation Criteria
1. **Communication Quality**: Specificity, clarity, inclusion of check-ins
2. **Boundary Respect**: Honoring limits without pressure or coercion
3. **Aftercare Integration**: Planning for post-scene care and follow-up
4. **Adaptability**: Adjusting consent approaches to partner needs
5. **Community Accountability**: Understanding bystander intervention duties
