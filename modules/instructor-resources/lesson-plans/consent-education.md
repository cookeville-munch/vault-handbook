# Lesson Plan Template: Consent Education for New Practitioners

## Core Principle Framework
- **Affirmative Consent = Ongoing Affirmative Consent**
- **No Assumptions = No Pressures**
- **"Your Kink Is Not My Kink And That's Okay" Integration**
- **Inclusive Consent = Accessible Consent for All Bodies & Minds**

## Session Breakdown

### 1. Introduction (45 minutes)
- **Warm-Up**: "Judgment-Free Fantasy Sharing" (communicate desires without approval)
- **Video Analysis**: Diverse consent negotiation examples (watch & discuss) - includes neurodivergent, disabled, and culturally diverse scenarios
- **Role-Play**: Consent negotiation scenarios (rotating partners) - scenarios covering sensory processing differences, mobility adaptations, cultural communication styles
- **Debrief**: Key takeaways, common pitfalls, inclusive adaptations

### 2. Skills Practice (90 minutes)
- **Skill Builder**: Consent agreement drafting (with rubric feedback) - templates include AAC-friendly, visual, and multi-language versions
- **Dynamic Practice**: Consent check-ins during simulated play - includes non-verbal systems, tactile signals, and processing time accommodations
- **Boundary Mapping**: Visual mapping of personal/partner boundaries - tactile and digital options for diverse accessibility needs
- **Digital Consent**: Email/text consent templates practice - screen-reader compatible, simplified language versions

### 3. Observation & Feedback (60 minutes)
- **Live Observation**: Watch experienced mentor negotiate with participant - mentors include neurodivergent and disabled practitioners
- **Feedback Circle**: Peer and mentor feedback using structured rubric - rubric includes inclusive communication criteria
- **Boundary Innovation**: Practice creative boundary communication techniques - AAC devices, sign language, tactile symbols, visual cards
- **Self-Assessment**: Update personal learning journal - includes inclusive practice reflection prompts

## Inclusive Consent Adaptations Toolkit

### Neurodivergent-Affirming Practices
- **Processing Time**: Built-in 10-30 second pauses after questions
- **Literal Language**: "May I touch your arm?" vs. "Want to play?"
- **AAC Support**: Full support for communication devices, sign language, written/typed negotiation
- **Sensory Check-Ins**: Number scales, colored cards, hand signals alongside verbal
- **Executive Function Supports**: Written checklists, visual schedules, step-by-step protocols

### Disability-Inclusive Consent Frameworks
- **Mobility Adaptations**: Consent positioning for wheelchair users, seated negotiation, transfer protocols
- **Sensory Disability Protocols**: Tactile communication systems, vibration alerts, Braille/large print agreements
- **Chronic Pain/Fatigue**: Energy budgeting in consent negotiations, spoon-aware scene planning
- **Cognitive/Intellectual Disability**: Supported decision-making models, concrete consent tools, ongoing assent monitoring

### Cultural Humility in Consent
- **Language Justice**: Materials in multiple languages, interpreter protocols, community-specific terminology
- **Intersectional Boundaries**: Understanding how race, gender, disability, class shape consent dynamics
- **Historical Trauma Awareness**: Racial, colonial, medical trauma intersections with consent
- **Community-Specific Protocols**: Cultural consent traditions, elder involvement, collective consent models

## Instructor Toolkit
- **Consent Script Gallery**: Sample dialogues for varied scenarios (neurodivergent, disabled, culturally diverse)
- **Boundary Mapping Template**: Visual mapping worksheet (tactile, digital, large print versions)
- **Consent Negotiation Checklist**: Step-by-step facilitation guide (with inclusive adaptations column)
- **Crisis Response Playbook**: For boundary violations or emergencies (includes disability-specific emergency protocols)
- **Escalation Protocols**: Clear escalation paths for different violation types (culturally informed)
- **Inclusive Communication Quick-Reference**: AAC basics, sign language consent signs, tactile symbol guide

## Evaluation Criteria
1. **Communication Quality**: Specificity, clarity, inclusion of check-ins, multi-modal communication
2. **Boundary Respect**: Honoring limits without pressure or coercion (including non-verbal limits)
3. **Aftercare Integration**: Planning for post-scene care and follow-up (accessible aftercare planning)
4. **Adaptability**: Adjusting consent approaches to partner needs (neurology, disability, culture, language)
5. **Community Accountability**: Understanding bystander intervention duties (intersectional bystander skills)
6. **Inclusive Practice**: Demonstrated competency in at least two inclusive consent adaptations
