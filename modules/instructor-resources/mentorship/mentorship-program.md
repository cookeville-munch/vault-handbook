# Mentorship Program Framework

## Program Overview
- **Purpose**: Structured peer mentorship for kink education
- **Duration**: 6-month cycles with quarterly check-ins
- **Structure**: Mentor-mentee pairs + cohort learning

## Mentor Requirements
- Minimum 2 years active practice
- Completed educator certification
- Commitment to 2 hours/month mentorship
- Ongoing supervision participation

## Mentee Onboarding
1. **Needs Assessment**: Skill gaps, learning goals, support needs
2. **Matching Process**: Compatibility survey + mentor availability
3. **Agreement Signing**: Expectations, boundaries, confidentiality
4. **First Meeting**: Goal setting, communication preferences, schedule

## Mentorship Activities
- **Monthly 1:1 Sessions**: Progress review, skill practice, Q&A
- **Quarterly Cohort Workshops**: Group learning, peer feedback
- **Resource Sharing**: Curated articles, videos, toolkits
- **Shadowing Opportunities**: Observe mentor in teaching/leading

## Documentation & Tracking
- **Session Logs**: Date, topics, action items, reflections
- **Progress Reports**: Quarterly summaries for program evaluation
- **Feedback Loops**: Anonymous surveys at 3 and 6 months
- **Completion Certification**: Portfolio review + mentor endorsement

## Support Systems
- **Mentor Community**: Monthly peer supervision circle
- **Program Coordinator**: Administrative support, conflict resolution
- **Resource Library**: Templates, guides, referral lists
- **Emergency Protocols**: Clear escalation paths for concerns

## Evaluation Metrics
- Mentee skill acquisition (self + mentor rated)
- Program satisfaction (both parties)
- Retention rates
- Community contribution post-mentorship

*Adapt framework to local community needs and resources.*
