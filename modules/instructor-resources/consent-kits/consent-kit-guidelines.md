## Kit Components
1. Core Documents
   - Consent agreement templates (visual/audio/emoji formats)
   - Safety protocol checklist
   - Aftercare agreement

2. Communication Tools
   - Signal cards (red/yellow/green)
   - Consent verification badges
   - Emergency contact templates

3. Digital Integration
   - QR code linking to digital consent forms
   - App integration guide (for tracking agreements)

## Development Stages
1. Basic Kit (3 essential components)
2. Advanced Kit (add digital elements + crisis integration)
3. Customizable Templates
