## 3. Risk-Specific Play Protocols

### 3.1 Blood Play Safety
- Hemoglobin Saturation Thresholds: Maintain SpO2 ≥94%
- Vasculature Access: 21 gauge needles max for arm veins

### 3.2 Oxygen Restriction Play

- Oxygen Administration: 15L medical tank with non-rebreather mask

### 3.3 Needle Play Safety Framework
- Sterilization: Biological indicator strips weekly
- Pain Calibration: Numeric Rating Scale (NRS) tracking
