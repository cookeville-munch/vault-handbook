## Key Takeaways

- **Immediate Response Kit** - Understanding and applying immediate response kit is essential for safe and effective practice
- **Documentation Templates** - Understanding and applying documentation templates is essential for safe and effective practice
- **Community Coordination** - Understanding and applying community coordination is essential for safe and effective practice

## Immediate Response Kit
- Emergency contact cards
- Scene safety checklist
- Communication protocol cards

## Documentation Templates
- Incident report form (digital & printable)
- Witness statement template
- Follow-up care checklist

## Community Coordination
- Crisis team activation flowchart
- Resource allocation matrix
- Communication channel guide

## Recovery Resources
- Trauma-informed care referral list
- Peer support scheduling template
- Community rebuilding timeline
