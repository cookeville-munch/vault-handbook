# Medical Safety for Senior Practitioners

## I. Chronic Condition Coordination

### Hypertension & Blood Pressure Monitoring
- **Pre-Scene Screening**:
  - Required: Sitting systolic <140mmHg / diastolic <90mmHg
  - Continuous monitoring during intense impact with pulse oximeter
- **Medication Interaction Checklist**:
  - Avoid nitrates with vasodilators (e.g., poppers + nitroglycerin) - fatal drop risk
  - Document all cardiac medications (beta-blockers, diuretics) on intake form
- **Activity Restrictions**:
  - Avoid Valsalva maneuvers (straining) for those on anticoagulants
  - Limit high-intensity impact to <10 minutes per session
  - Mandatory 15-minute cooldown after any scene exceeding 120mmHg systolic spike

### Diabetes Management
- **Pre-Scene Assessment**:
  - Blood glucose check within 60 minutes pre-session (target range 90-140 mg/dL)
  - Avoid intense activity during insulin peak (1-3 hours post-dose)
  - Always have glucose source (15g fast-acting carbs) immediately accessible
- **During Scene**:
  - Monitor for hypoglycemia symptoms (tremor, confusion, pallor)
  - Staff trained to recognize non-verbal distress cues (not reliant on verbal safeword)
  - Emergency protocol: 15g glucose + 1 copper gluconate tablet (if available)
- **Post-Scene Recovery**:
  - 20-minute observation period post-session
  - Hydration with electrolyte solution (not sugary drinks)

### Cardiovascular Conditions
- **Pre-Participation Clearance**:
  - Must have recent ECG and clearance from cardiologist for graded activity
  - Screen for: 
    - Coronary artery disease 
    - Valvular abnormalities
    - Arrhythmias requiring restrictions
- **Activity Modifications**:
  - Avoid intense breath play or rapid position changes
  - Use tilt tables for controlled hemodynamics testing if adapted
  - Monitor pulse response during activity (HR surge >30bpm = stop)
- **Pharmacological Interactions**:
  - Beta-blockers can blunt physiological distress signals – rely on non-physiological cues (pallor, pallor shift)
  - Avoid coupling with sildenafil derivatives during impact play (risk of sudden blood pressure shifts)

## II. Equipment Safety Adaptations

### Compression Gear Adjustments
- **Blood Pressure Monitoring**: 
  - Wireless arm cuffs with real-time alerts (>5% deviation)
  - Visual/audio alerts synced to consent interface for silent alerts
- **Female Anatomy Considerations**:
  - Compression garments must not restrict pelvic blood flow during suspension
  - Use gender-specific pressure mapping studies (Harvard study refs)
- **Heat Generation**: 
  - Phase-change fabric layers activate at body+2°C
  - Heat duration limited to 8-minute cycles with automatic cutoff

## II. Joint & Bone Health Protocols

### Osteoporosis Precautions
- **Load Limits**: Max 15kg downward force on lumbar spine during suspension
- **Position Avoidance**: Full inversion (head-down) prohibited
- **Bone Density Screening**: Mandatory DEXA scan if T-score < -2.5
- **Injury Response**:
  - Immediate decompression within 60 seconds of pain escalation
  - Orthopedic consultation within 24 hours mandatory
  - Community restriction from advanced suspension until clearance

### Fragile Bone Management
- **Adaptive Harness Designs**:
  - 3D-printed padding conforming to vertebral contours
  - Elastic load-distribution panels (0.5kg weight)
  - Emergency-servo release (10N threshold)
- **Thermal Control**: 
  - Warm core preconditioning (37°C for 10min pre-session)
  - Anti-frostbite gloves for radial nerve protection during rigger work

## III. Cognitive/Memory Support Integration

### Consent Scheme Adaptations
- **Tiered Consent Markers**: 
  - ✔️ (yes) ➖ (pause) ✖️ (stop) 
  - Visual scaling: size ∝ importance of boundary
- **Process Walks**: 
  - "Step 1: Choose from 3 pre-approved scenes"
  - "Step 2: Circle preferred intensity level on chart"
  - "Step 3: Nurse confirms understanding with ✓"
- **Memory Reinforcement**:
  - Post-session printed card with: 
    - Agreed boundaries 
    - Emergency contacts  
    - Aftercare plan steps
  - Take-home QR code linking to audio recall of scene

### Trauma-Informed Review Process
- 24-hour check-in call (voluntary)
- Written boundary consent form returned via encrypted upload
- Option to mark boundaries as "Review in 30 days" if processing needed
- No abandonment of practice – redirect to community support circles
