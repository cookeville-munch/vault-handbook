# Skill Tracker Framework

## Skill Categories
**A. Technical Skills**
- Basic impact techniques (single arm, partner, flying)
- Advanced bondage (basic, intermediate, complex)
- Sensation play (heat, cold, vibration, pressure)
- Position play (static, dynamic, suspension)

**B. Relational Skills**
- Negotiating hard/soft limits
- Creating consent agreements
- Communication cue systems
- Aftercare planning
- Body positivity modeling

**C. Safety & Risk Management**
- Scene safety protocols
- Equipment inspection standards
- Emergency response procedures
- Medical awareness (common conditions)
- Risk-benefit assessment

**D. Educational Skills**
- Audience engagement techniques
- Accessibility accommodations
- Cultural competency considerations
- Trauma-informed facilitation
