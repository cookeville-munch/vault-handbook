# Practices Journal Template

## Entry Structure
1. **Date & Session Info**
   - Date, time, location
   - Type of practice (session, scene, workshop)
   - Participants (roles, names/identifiers)

2. **Pre-Practice Prep**
   - Goals and boundaries
   - Materials/tools needed
   - Safety checks completed

3. **During Practice**
   - Activities performed
   - Consent checkpoints
   - Communication cues used
   - Emotional state tracking

4. **Aftercare & Debrief**
   - Aftercare performed
   - Emotional processing
   - Feedback received
   - Lessons learned

5. **Reflection**
   - What worked well
   - Areas for improvement
   - Insights for future practices
   - Skill development notes

## Skill Tracking
- **Current Skill Level**: Rate 1-5 for specific techniques
  - Impact play
  - Bondage
  - Sensation play
  - Negotiation
- **Strengths Identified**
- **Growth Targets**
- **Practice Schedule**

## Resource Connections
- **Related Materials**: Related guides, videos, templates
- **Mentor Scope**: Mentor check-in date
- **Recommended Reading**: Articles/resources

## Format Guide
- Length: 1-3 pages per entry
- Language: Accessible, non-judgmental, descriptive
- Sections: Clearly labeled with headers
- Visual Aids: Optionally include diagrams or checklists

*Purpose: Track growth, ensure consistent practice standards, support skill development, and facilitate reflective learning.*
