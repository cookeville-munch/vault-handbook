# Crisis Management Scenarios

## Scenario 1: Boundary Violation During Scene
**Profile**: During a negotiated flogging scene, the recipient uses their safeword but the giver continues for 8 more strikes.  
**Context**: Giver reports mishearing due to loud music; recipient experiences distress and flashback.  

**Response Protocol**:
1. **Immediate Intervention**: Safety monitor notices distress, uses pre-agreed "time out" hand signal
2. **Scene Cessation**: All activity stops, lights brought to safe level
3. **Separation & Care**: Recipient guided to quiet space with trauma-informed supporter; giver separated for remains with accountability buddy
4. **Medical Check**: Vital signs assessed (pulse, respiration, emotional state)
5. **Documentation**: Incident logged with timestamps, witness statements, device logs if applicable
6. **Follow-Up Path**: 
   - 24-hour check-in from community support lead
   - Option for mediated conversation or restorative process
   - Access to kink-aware therapist if desired
   - Temporary activity restriction for giver pending review

**Prevention Upgrades**:
- Music volume limits established (<60 dB during negotiation/check-ins)
- Mandatory visual confirmation of safeword use
- Post-scene debrief requirement for all participants

## Scenario 2: Medical Emergency in Suspension
**Profile**: Participant in partial suspension reports sudden chest pain and shortness of breath.  
**Context**: Known history of hypertension, took medication 2 hours prior.  

**Response Protocol**:
1. **Emergency Activation**: Safety lead calls 911 with specific details (suspension, possible cardiac)
2. **Immediate Care**: 
   - Controlled descent using emergency release system
   - Place in recovery position, monitor airway
   - Administer aspirin if available and not contraindicated
   - Prepare AED (if trained)
3. **Scene Security**: Clear area, maintain privacy with screens
4. **Information Transfer**: Provide EMS with: 
   - Exact substances used (lubricants, etc.)
   - Time of onset, interventions performed
   - Contact for community medical liaison
5. **Community Notification**: 
   - Discreet alert to event organizer (no details shared)
   - Prepared statement for attendees if needed
   - Privacy maintained for medical details
6. **After-Action Review**: 
   - Medical follow-up coordinated
   - Event medical plan reviewed (onsite EMT? AED availability?)
   - Consent form updated with cardiac risk disclosure

## Scenario 3: Online Harassment Escalation
**Profile**: Community member receives increasing hostile messages after sharing educational content about edge play.  
**Context**: Messages progress from criticism to doxxing threats and violent language.  

**Response Protocol**:
1. **Immediate Safety**: 
   - Advise member to enable 2FA, review privacy settings
   - Offer temporary alternate identity/profile options
   - Document all communications (screenshots, timestamps)
2. **Platform Engagement**: 
   - Report to platform using harassment/copyright/violence categories
   - Request content removal and account suspension of aggressor
   - Request IP information if legally permissible
3. **Community Support**: 
   - Activate buddy system for check-ins (2x daily)
   - Offer digital security audit of member's accounts
   - Provide list of legal resources (cyberlaw, anti-doxxing)
4. **Legal Pathways**: 
   - Consult with electronic frontier foundation or similar
   - Consider cease-and-desist if identifiable aggressor
   - Document for potential restraining order
5. **Community Message**: 
   - General statement about zero tolerance for harassment
   - Share resources for digital safety and support
   - No naming of individuals without consent
6. **Prevention Measures**: 
   - Workshop on online safety and privacy settings
   - Update community guidelines with clear harassment definitions
   - Establish rapid response team for digital incidents

## Scenario 4: Venue Power Failure During Event
**Profile**: Lights and ventilation fail midway through a play party with 50 attendees.  
**Context**: Older building, no backup generator, outdoor temperature 85°F.  

**Response Protocol**:
1. **Immediate Safety**: 
   - Announce pause via pre-arranged signal (3 short whistle blasts)
   - Guide participants to well-lit, ventilated areas (lobby/patio)
   - Check on anyone in compromised positions (suspension, bondage)
2. **Medical Triage**: 
   - Assess for heat-related distress, panic attacks
   - Provide water, cooling towels, seated rest
   - Monitor vulnerable participants (elderly, medical conditions)
3. **Resource Mobilization**: 
   - Contact venue manager for ETA on power restoration
   - Deploy battery-operated fans if available
   - Consider early termination if >30 minute outage
4. **Communication**: 
   - Clear, calm updates every 10 minutes
   - Offer discreet departure assistance if needed
   - Preserve dignity: no speculation about cause
5. **After-Care Considerations**: 
   - Extended aftercare time available for those needing it
   - Offer community transport for those uncomfortable traveling alone
   - Follow-up check-ins for next 24 hours (heat exhaustion can delay)
6. **Future Planning**: 
   - Require venues to provide outage protocols
   - Recommend portable power solutions for future events
   - Update emergency checklist with power failure steps
