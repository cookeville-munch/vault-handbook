# Accessibility Practice Scenarios

## Scenario 1: Non-Verbal Negotiation for AAC User
**Profile**: Participant uses augmentative communication device (speech-generating app).  
**Goal**: Negotiate impact play scene using device + visual supports.  

**Tools**:
- Pre-loaded consent vocabulary: "yes/no/maybe/stop/check-in"
- Symbol-based activity menu (pictures of implements + intensity scales)
- Time-delay setting: 8 seconds between question and response
- Backup: physical symbol cards on lanyard

**Facilitation Protocol**:
- Partner reads options aloud while pointing to symbols
- Wait full delay before repeating
- Record responses with timestamps
- Confirm each choice twice ("You selected 'flogger, medium'. Correct?")

**Safety Override**:
- Dedicated "EMERGENCY STOP" button on device (large, red, single-press)
- Partner carries identical physical button

## Scenario 2: Sensory-Friendly Rope Workshop
**Profile**: Group of 6 neurodivergent participants with varied sensory profiles.  
**Goal**: Teach basic chest harness with minimized sensory overwhelm.  

**Environment Modifications**:
- Dimmable lighting (participants control individual stations)
- Noise-canceling headphones available at each station
- Weighted lap pads for grounding
- Scent-free policy enforced (no oils/perfumes)

**Instruction Adaptations**:
- Video loop of each step (no narration)
- Color-coded rope segments for visual sequencing
- Tactile "cheat sheet" with rope textures & knot samples
- Option to practice on mannequin first

**Check-In System**:
- Emoji cards: 😊 (good) 😐 (neutral) 😣 (overwhelmed)
- Scheduled pauses: 10 min work / 5 min sensory break
- Private decompression room with fidget tools

## Scenario 3: Mobility-Inclusive Play Party
**Profile**: Public event with 20+ attendees including wheelchair users and ambulatory aids.  
**Goal**: Ensure all play spaces and social areas accessible.  

**Venue Layout**:
- 42" minimum pathways throughout
- Adjustable-height play stations (manual cranks)
- Transfer benches near suspension points
- Designated "rest pods" with charging stations

**Activity Adaptations**:
- Seated impact options (cushioned receiving benches)
- Grounded suspension alternatives (floor-anchored rigs)
- Partner matching considering mobility compatibility
- Clear signage: tactile + high-contrast + Braille

**Support Staff**:
- 2 accessibility coordinators (identifiable vests)
- Pre-event accessibility walkthrough video available
- On-site equipment lending: grips, straps, reachers
