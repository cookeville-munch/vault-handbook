# Pre-Session Safety Checklist for Instructors

## Environmental Safety Verification
- [ ] Room temperature maintained between 68-75°F (20-24°C)
- [ ] Adequate ventilation with fresh air exchange
- [ ] Clear emergency exits (minimum 2 unobstructed paths)
- [ ] Non-slip flooring surfaces throughout activity areas
- [ ] Adequate lighting (minimum 50 lux general, task lighting as needed)
- [ ] Temperature-controlled water sources available
- [ ] First aid kit accessible and fully stocked
- [ ] Fire extinguisher present and inspected within last year
- [ ] Defibrillator/AED available if electrical play anticipated

## Equipment Inspection Protocol
- [ ] All rope inspected for fraying, discoloration, or weak spots
- [ ] Hardware (carabiners, shackles, rings) checked for cracks/deformation
- [ ] Impact implements examined for splinters, cracks, or instability
- [ ] Electrical devices tested with multimeter for shorts/ground faults
- [ ] Medical equipment sterilized per CDC guidelines
- [ ] Safety shears/emergency release tools readily accessible
- [ ] Weight-bearing equipment load-tested within last 6 months
- [ ] All equipment cleaned and disinfected between uses

## Participant Screening & Preparation
- [ ] Pre-session health questionnaire completed by all participants
- [ ] Medication interactions reviewed for all prescription drugs
- [ ] Alcohol/substance use prohibited within 8 hours of session
- [ ] Recent injuries, surgeries, or medical conditions disclosed
- [ ] Pregnancy status confirmed for applicable participants
- [ ] Diabetes/blood sugar levels checked if impact play planned
- [ ] Cardiovascular clearance obtained for high-intensity activities
- [ ] Allergy information documented (latex, metals, lubricants, etc.)

## Consent & Communication Systems
- [ ] Verbal and non-verbal safewords established for each participant
- [ ] Check-in intervals predetermined and communicated
- [ ] Emergency signals reviewed (distress gestures, colored cards)
- [ ] Communication aids available (ASL interpreter, picture boards)
- [ ] Language barriers addressed with translation services if needed
- [ ] Cognitive accommodations prepared for neurodivergent participants
- [ ] Trauma triggers discussed and avoided where possible
- [ ] Aftercare needs and preferences documented in advance

## Emergency Response Readiness
- [ ] Emergency contact list posted and accessible
- [ ] Nearest hospital with trauma capability identified
- [ ] EMS response time confirmed (<8 minutes for urban areas)
- [ ] Designated safety monitor assigned for each active scene
- [ ] Incident report forms available and understood by staff
- [ ] Privacy protocol established for sensitive situations
- [ ] Debrief space prepared for post-incident processing
- [ ] Legal counsel contact information readily available

## Documentation & Liability Protection
- [ ] Participant waivers signed and dated
- [ ] Medical consent forms completed for minors/vulnerable adults
- [ ] Session-specific risk assessments documented
- [ ] Equipment maintenance logs updated pre-session
- [ ] Instructor certifications and credentials verified current
- [ ] Photography/video release forms obtained if applicable
- [ ] Data protection protocols established for sensitive information
- [ ] Post-session follow-up schedule communicated to participants
