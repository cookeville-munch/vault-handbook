# Curriculum Map for Kink Education Program

## Eight-Week Progressive Learning Schedule
**Week 1-2**: Foundations of Consent & Safety
- Core readings: Consent principles, trauma-informed care, neurodivergent-affirming negotiation
- Practical: Basic negotiation exercises, safety protocol drills, disability-inclusive safety audits

**Week 3-4**: Technical Skills Foundations
- Rope suspension basics, medical play fundamentals, sensory play methodologies
- Lab: Equipment inspection, basic tie-tying, sensation play material selection

**Week 5-6**: Advanced Practice Techniques
- Sensation play choreography, advanced restraint methods, impact play safety architecture
- Role-play: High-intensity scenario simulations, inclusive scene design case studies

**Week 7-8**: Community Leadership & Ethics
- Mentorship frameworks, public education design, cultural humility in kink spaces
- Final project: Design a community workshop with accessibility audit

## Competency Thresholds
- Level 1: 90%+ completion of prerequisite modules (including special populations)
- Level 2: Demonstrated proficiency in 3 core techniques + inclusive adaptation skills
- Level 3: Successful mentorship of 2+ newcomers + accessibility audit completion
- Level 4: Peer-reviewed curriculum contribution with intersectional analysis

## Inter-Module Dependencies
- Completion of Module 1 (Consent & Safety) required before Module 2 (Technical Skills)
- Module 3 (Advanced Practice) requires documented safety audit + neurodivergent case study
- Module 4 (Community Leadership) requires 10 hours community service + cultural humility assessment
- Special Populations module must be integrated across all weeks

## Assessment Integration
- Weekly knowledge quizzes (auto-generated, including inclusive practice scenarios)
- Monthly practical evaluations (peer-reviewed with accessibility criteria)
- Final portfolio review (community panel with diverse representation)
- Neurodivergent communication assessment (Week 2)
- Disability adaptation demonstration (Week 4)
