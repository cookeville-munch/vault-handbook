# Sharps Play Safety Protocols

## Overview
This module provides expert-level guidance on sharps play techniques with emphasis on medical safety, risk assessment, and harm reduction. It covers advanced techniques for experienced practitioners who have mastered foundational and intermediate kink practices.

## Core Principles
- Medical safety hierarchy and aseptic technique
- Risk-benefit analysis for sharps play activities
- Informed consent and ongoing communication protocols
- Emergency medical response planning

## Techniques

- Skin preparation and aseptic field setup
- Controlled penetration techniques and depth management
- Pattern work and artistic considerations
- Safe removal and aftercare procedures

## Case Studies

- Scenario: Participant developed localized infection following sharps scene

- Outcome: Elimination of post-procedure infections through improved hygiene

## Safety Protocols
- Medical history screening and contraindication assessment
- Equipment sterilization and single-use protocols
- Bleeding control and wound management procedures
- Emergency medical services coordination plan
- Special considerations for diabetes, immunocompromised states, and bleeding disorders
