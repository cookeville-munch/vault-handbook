# Community Crisis Response Plan

## Overview
This document outlines the community's coordinated response to crises affecting multiple members or the community as a whole. It complements individual crisis protocols with community-level activation procedures.

## Activation Criteria
Community crisis response is triggered by:
- Multiple simultaneous individual crises (3+ within 24 hours)
- Community-wide event incidents (play parties, workshops, conferences)
- External threats (legal actions, media exposure, platform censorship)
- Infrastructure failures (venue loss, communication platform shutdown)
- Community leader/elder health emergencies

## Response Team Structure

### Crisis Leadership (3-5 members)
- **Community Coordinator**: Overall response coordination, external communications
- **Safety Lead**: Physical/psychological safety of affected members
- **Resources Lead**: Mobilizes community resources, funds, volunteers
- **Communications Lead**: Internal updates, external statements, media liaison
- **Documentation Lead**: Records all actions, decisions, outcomes

### Extended Response Network
- **Peer Supporters**: Trained volunteers for emotional support (10-20 people)
- **Legal Advisors**: Pro bono attorneys familiar with community issues
- **Medical Professionals**: Kink-aware therapists, doctors, nurses
- **Technical Team**: Platform admins, security experts, backup coordinators
- **Venue Partners**: Alternative space providers for displaced events

## Activation Protocol

### Phase 1: Recognition & Alert (0-30 minutes)
1. **Trigger Identification**: Any response team member identifies community crisis criteria
2. **Alert Activation**: Secure signal to all response team members (encrypted group chat, phone tree)
3. **Initial Assessment**: Rapid evaluation of scope, severity, immediate needs
4. **Leadership Convening**: Emergency video call within 15 minutes of alert

### Phase 2: Stabilization (30 minutes - 4 hours)
1. **Safety Triage**: Identify members at immediate risk, deploy peer supporters
2. **Resource Deployment**: Activate emergency funds, alternative venues, communication channels
3. **Member Communication**: Clear, calm update to community via multiple channels
4. **External Coordination**: Contact legal/medical/technical partners as needed
5. **Documentation Start**: Begin incident log with timestamps, decisions, actions

### Phase 3: Sustained Response (4 hours - 72 hours)
1. **Daily Briefings**: Response team syncs, adjust strategies
2. **Member Check-ins**: Systematic outreach to affected subgroups
3. **Resource Management**: Track fund usage, volunteer hours, supply levels
4. **Communication Cadence**: Regular updates (every 4-6 hours) to community
6. **Adaptation**: Adjust response based on evolving situation

### Phase 4: Recovery & Integration (72 hours - 30 days)
1. **Impact Assessment**: Survey community needs, document lessons learned
2. **Transition Planning**: Shift from crisis response to ongoing support
3. **Community Healing**: Facilitated processing spaces, memorial if needed
4. **System Improvements**: Update protocols, train new responders, replenish resources
5. **After-Action Report**: Comprehensive review within 30 days

## Specific Crisis Scenarios

### Legal/Police Action
- **Immediate**: Legal advisors contacted, member rights cards distributed
- **Communication**: Pre-approved holding statements, no speculation
- **Support**: Legal defense fund activation, attorney referrals
- **Long-term**: Policy advocacy, community legal education

### Platform Censorship/Deplatforming
- **Technical**: Activate backup platforms, migrate critical data
- **Communication**: Guide members to alternative spaces, preserve connections
- **Advocacy**: Coordinate with digital rights organizations
- **Resilience**: Accelerate decentralized infrastructure development

### Venue Loss/Event Cancellation
- **Immediate**: Alternative venue network activation
- **Financial**: Refund/rebooking process, emergency event fund
- **Communication**: Clear attendee instructions, preserve event purpose
- **Community**: Transform into distributed micro-events if needed

### Community Leader Crisis
- **Succession**: Activate predetermined leadership continuity plan
- **Support**: Immediate professional care for affected leader
- **Communication**: Respect privacy while acknowledging community impact
- **Stability**: Ensure operational continuity, prevent power vacuums

## Resource Management

### Emergency Funds
- **Community Crisis Fund**: $10,000 minimum reserve, replenished quarterly
- **Rapid Access**: Two signatories required, 24-hour disbursement
- **Tracking**: Transparent logging, monthly community reports

### Volunteer Management
- **Response Roster**: 50+ trained volunteers with skill tags
- **Shift System**: 4-hour rotations with mandatory breaks
- **Support**: Debriefing, trauma-informed supervision, appreciation events

### Physical Resources
- **Venue Network**: 5+ pre-vetted alternative spaces with contacts
- **Supply Cache**: First aid, comfort items, communication equipment
- **Transportation**: Volunteer driver pool, rideshare partnerships

## Communication Protocols

### Internal Channels
- **Primary**: Encrypted group chat (Signal/Element) for response team
- **Secondary**: Phone tree for critical alerts if primary fails
- **Coordination**: Shared documents (CryptPad/Nextcloud) for real-time collaboration
- **Documentation**: Structured incident log with timestamps, decisions, actions

### External Communication
- **Community Updates**: Clear, compassionate, factual, regular cadence
- **Media Statements**: Pre-approved templates, single spokesperson
- **Partner Notifications**: Allies, venues, professionals informed as appropriate
- **Privacy Protection**: No member names/details without explicit consent

### Post-Crisis Communication
- **Impact Report**: Transparent summary of what happened, response, outcomes
- **Lessons Learned**: Shared with community (anonymized), integrated into training
- **Appreciation**: Public recognition of responders, volunteers, supporters
- **Ongoing Support**: Clear pathways for continued help

## Training & Preparedness

### Quarterly Drills
- **Tabletop Exercises**: Scenario walkthroughs with response team
- **Communication Tests**: Verify all channels, backup systems
- **Resource Audits**: Check fund levels, supply caches, contact lists
- **New Responder Training**: Quarterly onboarding for volunteer pool

### Annual Full-Scale Exercise
- **Community-Wide Simulation**: Multi-hour scenario with all hands
- **After-Action Review**: Comprehensive debrief, protocol updates
- **Cross-Community Coordination**: Practice with allied communities
- **Infrastructure Stress Test**: Backup systems under load

## Legal & Ethical Framework

### Decision-Making Authority
- **Emergency Powers**: Response team has authority to deploy resources, make commitments up to $5,000
- **Community Oversight**: All actions reviewed at next community meeting
- **Accountability**: Transparent logging, accessible to community members

### Privacy & Confidentiality
- **Need-to-Know Basis**: Information shared only with relevant responders
- **Data Protection**: Encrypted storage, auto-deletion after 90 days unless legal hold
- **Member Rights**: Access to own data, correction, deletion requests honored

### Mandatory Reporting Compliance
- **Clear Protocols**: When to involve authorities, how to protect members
- **Legal Counsel**: Immediate consultation for reporting decisions
- **Community Values**: Balance safety with autonomy, minimize harm
