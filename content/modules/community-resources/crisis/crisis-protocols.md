# Crisis Response Protocols

## 3-Phase Emergency Activation

### Phase 1: Immediate Response (0-5 minutes)
- **Safety Check**: Verify physical safety, secure environment
- **Grounding Protocol**: 5-4-3-2-1 technique for emotional regulation
- **Emergency Contact Activation**: Pre-designated support network notification

### Phase 2: Stabilization (5-30 minutes)
- **Basic Needs Assessment**: Hydration, temperature regulation, medication access
- **Communication Protocol**: Establish safe word/check-in rhythm
- **Aftercare Initiation**: Begin appropriate aftercare sequence

### Phase 3: Follow-up (30 minutes - 72 hours)
- **Debrief Process**: Structured reflection with trained facilitator
- **Resource Referral**: Connect to mental health, legal, or medical services
- **Community Support Activation**: Mobilize peer support circles

## Incident Response Workflow
1. **Classification**: Immediate physical danger, psychological crisis, boundary violation
2. **Escalation Path**: Peer support → crisis team → professional intervention
3. **Documentation Requirements**: Timestamped logs, participant consent status, action taken
4. **Post-Incident Review**: 48-hour follow-up, community impact assessment

## Crisis Support Roles
- **First Responders**: Trained volunteers with crisis de-escalation certification
- **Mental Health Professionals**: Licensed therapists with kink-affirming training
- **Community Mediators**: Neutral facilitators for conflict resolution
- **Legal Advisors**: Pro bono attorneys specializing in sexual rights

## Community Notification System
- **Automated Alerts**: Secure messaging to crisis team members
- **Public Advisory**: General safety notice without identifying details
- **Resource Dissemination**: Share crisis hotline information
- **Follow-up Check-ins**: Structured outreach to affected participants
