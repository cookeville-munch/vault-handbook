## Cultural Humility Foundations

### Core Practices

1. **Lifelong Learning**
   - Mandatory annual bias training with community reviewers
   - Budget allocation for marginalized instructor stipends

2. **Power Analysis**
   - Quarterly reflection circles on dominance dynamics in sessions
   - Public accountability logs for instructors' intersectional blind spots

3. **Institutional Accountability**
   - 30% of teaching slots reserved for marginalized community members
