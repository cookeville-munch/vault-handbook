## Implementation Frameworks
1. Knowledge Preservation Protocol
   - Yearly knowledge audits
   - Legacy contributor recognition
   - Version-controlled documentation workflows

2. Intergenerational Handoff Process
   - Mentorship pairing system
   - Oral history collection guidelines
   - Multi-generational scenario training

3. Digital Archiving Standards
   - Metadata schema for all materials
   - Preservation-grade storage recommendations
   - Accessibility compliance requirements
