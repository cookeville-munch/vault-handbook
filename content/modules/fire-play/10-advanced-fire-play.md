# Fire Play Safety & Techniques

## Overview
This module covers advanced fire play techniques with emphasis on safety protocols, risk mitigation, and proper technique execution for experienced practitioners. It builds upon foundational knowledge to provide specialized skills in temperature-based sensation play.

## Core Principles
- Fire safety hierarchy and risk assessment
- Thermal regulation and monitoring
- Consent and communication protocols for temperature play
- Emergency response procedures

## Techniques
- Controlled flame introduction and extinguishing methods
- Temperature gradient application
- Sensory progression exercises
- Partner coordination and safety monitoring

## Case Studies
### Case Study 1: Thermal Injury Prevention
- Scenario: Participant with reduced sensation experienced accidental burns during temperature play
- Solution: Implementation of thermal mapping and graduated exposure protocols
- Outcome: Prevention of further injury through improved safety measures

### Case Study 2: Equipment Calibration Failure
- Scenario: Improperly calibrated heating equipment caused inconsistent temperature application
- Solution: Standardized equipment testing and calibration procedures
- Outcome: Consistent temperature delivery and reduced risk of injury

## Safety Protocols
- Pre-play thermal baseline assessment
- Real-time temperature monitoring with automatic alerts
- Emergency cooling procedures and first aid
- Post-play tissue integrity verification
- Special considerations for medical conditions and medications

## Testing
- Practical demonstration of temperature control techniques
- Written assessment on safety protocols and emergency procedures
- Partner feedback on communication and safety awareness
- Skills checklist completion for competency verification
